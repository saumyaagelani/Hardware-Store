# Information required from the client

Everything the store owner needs to provide before the website can go live. It is written so it can be forwarded as-is, for example by Aditya to the client.

- **Part A** is required before production.
- **Part B** is optional and can be added after launch.

Everything on the demo site today (business name "Northline Building Supply", address, phone numbers, emails, hours, products, prices, brands, photos, policies) is **placeholder content**.

**How to send it:** a shared folder (Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive) with one subfolder per section below. For products, one spreadsheet is best. A template can be provided on request.

---

## A. REQUIRED BEFORE PRODUCTION

### A1. Approval of the prototype
- [ ] Written approval of the Stage 1 demo, **or** one consolidated list of changes (what page, what to change, screenshots if possible)
- [ ] Confirmation of the main user journeys: shopping and checkout, quotes, contractor program, customer account, admin dashboard
- [ ] Anything shown in the demo that you do **not** want on the live site

### A2. Business identity
- [ ] Legal business name and the trading name to show on the website
- [ ] Logo files: vector (SVG, AI or EPS) preferred, otherwise high-resolution PNG with transparent background; light and dark versions if you have them
- [ ] Brand guidelines if any (already applied: the reference dark theme — #1D211C, #262B24, #32382F, #F3F1E9, #B2B7AA, #40463C, #CDD7BD — with the approved #F5B82E yellow accent)
- [ ] Business number / HST registration number (for receipts and invoices)

### A3. Contact details and locations
- [ ] Main phone number, text/SMS number (if different), and WhatsApp (if used)
- [ ] Public email address(es): general, sales, orders, contractor accounts
- [ ] Showroom/warehouse street address(es) and Google Maps location
- [ ] Opening hours for each location, including weekends and holiday closures
- [ ] Which staff inbox(es) should receive new-order, new-quote, contractor-application and contact-form alerts

### A4. Pickup
- [ ] Pickup location(s): address, hours, entrance or door, parking or loading notes
- [ ] How soon orders are usually ready for pickup (same day, next business day, etc.)
- [ ] What the customer must bring (order number, ID)

### A5. Delivery
- [ ] Areas you deliver to: list of cities or postal-code prefixes (e.g. L4, L5, M)
- [ ] Delivery fee for each area, and any free-delivery threshold
- [ ] Typical delivery lead time for each area
- [ ] Rules for large or heavy items (doors, vanities, shower bases, bulk flooring): when is the fee "to be confirmed"?
- [ ] Whether customers can choose a delivery date or time window
- [ ] Areas you do **not** deliver to, and what to tell those customers

### A6. Taxes
- [ ] Do you only sell in Ontario (13% HST), or also to other provinces?
- [ ] Any tax-exempt customers (e.g. First Nations, resellers with exemption certificates)?

### A7. Products and categories
- [ ] Confirmation of the 11 categories and their subcategories (Vinyl Flooring, Stairs, Doors, Locks, Shower Bases, Shower Doors, Shower Accessories, Toilet Seats, Vanities, Plumbing, WPC Wall Panels) and anything to add or remove
- [ ] Product spreadsheet with one row per product (or per variant):
  - Name, SKU, category and subcategory, brand
  - Short and full description, key features
  - **Retail price, sale price (and end date), contractor price**
  - Selling unit (each, box, sq. ft., set, piece, pack) and minimum order
  - For flooring: square feet per box (used by the calculator)
  - Variants (colour, size, finish, handing) and any price difference per variant
  - Stock quantity or status (in stock, low stock, out of stock, special order), lead time for special orders
  - Specifications (dimensions, thickness, wear layer, material, finish, certifications)
  - Installation notes and warranty
- [ ] Which products should show **"Request a Quote"** instead of a price
- [ ] Which products are **"contractors only"** (price visible only to approved contractors)
- [ ] Product photos for every product: high resolution, preferably on a plain background, plus room/lifestyle photos if available. **You must own the rights** or have supplier permission. We will not copy photos from other websites.
- [ ] Spec sheets, installation guides and warranty PDFs from manufacturers

### A8. Contractor program
- [ ] Who qualifies (trades, business registration, minimum volume?)
- [ ] What information to collect on the application (the demo asks for company name, business type, address, HST/business number, trade licence or registration number, years in business, estimated monthly purchases, website and a short description of the work)
- [ ] Who approves applications, and how quickly
- [ ] One contractor price level for all, or several tiers or customer-specific pricing?
- [ ] Contractor terms: payment terms, net-30 accounts, confidentiality of pricing

### A9. Payments
- [ ] Which payment methods to accept: credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Interac e-Transfer, pay in store, financing
- [ ] Payment provider preference (Stripe is recommended), or your existing bank/merchant provider (Moneris, Helcim, Square…)
- [ ] The **account owner** must create the payment account in the business name. We need a developer or API access invitation, never your password
- [ ] Interac e-Transfer: receiving email address, security question/answer policy (or auto-deposit), and how long to hold orders awaiting payment
- [ ] Deposits for special orders? Full payment up front?
- [ ] Refund and cancellation process

### A10. Policies and legal (final wording)
- [ ] Returns and exchanges (time limit, restocking fee, opened boxes, special orders, custom cuts)
- [ ] Delivery policy (inspection on delivery, damages, missed deliveries, curbside or inside)
- [ ] Warranty policy
- [ ] Privacy policy (PIPEDA): ideally reviewed by your lawyer
- [ ] Terms and conditions of sale
- [ ] Contractor program terms
- [ ] Consent wording for marketing emails (CASL)

### A11. Domain and email
- [ ] Domain name you want to use (and whether you already own it)
- [ ] Access to the domain registrar or DNS (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare…), or a contact who can add DNS records
- [ ] The email address the website should send from (e.g. orders@yourdomain.ca)
- [ ] Who manages your current email (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365…), because email authentication records must not break it

### A12. Hosting and accounts
- [ ] Agreement on hosting provider and plan (monthly cost billed to the business)
- [ ] Who owns the accounts (recommended: the business owns them and adds the developer as a team member)

### A13. Admin users
- [ ] Names and emails of staff who need admin access
- [ ] What each person may manage: products, pricing, inventory, orders, quotes, customers, contractor approvals, website content, settings

### A14. Homepage and core content
- [ ] Homepage headline and sub-text (or approval of the demo wording)
- [ ] About Us text (history, team, values) and photos of the showroom/warehouse/team
- [ ] Current promotions or sale for launch (if any)
